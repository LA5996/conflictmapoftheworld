Privacy

Short version: this site does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and it does not ask for or store personal data.

What happens when you visit

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web server, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the pages and may keep it briefly in security logs. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Live earthquakes. On the Natural disasters tab, your browser requests the public earthquake feed directly from the US Geological Survey (earthquake.usgs.gov), which therefore sees your IP address.
  • Map outlines. Country shapes are served from this site. Only if that copy fails to load does your browser fetch a backup copy from the jsDelivr content network.
  • Email. If you write to us, your message is handled in Gmail (Google) and used only to reply to you.
  • Links. Source links take you to other websites, which have their own privacy policies.

What we do not do

No cookies, no local tracking, no analytics, no third-party fonts, no ads, no accounts, and no forms. We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect any.

Contact

For questions about this notice or to report an error, email conflictmapoftheworld@gmail.com. If you email us, we use your message only to reply and do not share it. This notice was last updated on 27 November 2026.
